## This section covers questions to tackle from recruiter after getting a job

### if you still join our company if you receive a better offer elsewhere?
A. You can say something along these lines:
 
1. "Between a better offer and a more suitable offer, I would always choose the latter. For me, a suitable offer isn't just about numbers-it's about finding a place where my goals, values, and the work environment are in harmony. This way, even if I don't go for the better offer, I'll convert this opportunity into the better offer by excelling at my work.
 
2. I believe that this company aligns more closely with my professional aspirations and values (Insert 2-3 specific values). Throughout the interview process, I've been impressed by the culture here. I've really enjoyed the conversations I've had (Mention 2-3 specific conversations), and it's clear that the people who work here are both passionate and welcoming.
 
3. For me, working with a team that values collaboration, growth, and mutual respect is paramount. People matter more than just a title or a paycheck, and I've found that this team embodies those principles.
 
In conclusion, I would love to join your team. I'm confident that with the right package, I can exceed expectations and truly excel in this role."
