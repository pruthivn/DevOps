

### 1. HR tells they are moving forward with another candidate?
A. mail template:
```text

Hi [Name],
 
Thank you for letting me know, and for the time your team invested in my application.
 
While I'm disappointed, I completely understand. I was genuinely excited about [Company] and the [Role] team.
 
Two quick asks:
 
1. If any feedback comes to mind on where I could've been a stronger fit, I'd value it - I'm always looking to grow.
 
2. If a similar role opens up down the line, I'd love to be considered. I'll stay connected either way.
 
Wishing you and the team a smooth onboarding for the new hire.
 
Best, 
[Your Name]"
 
```

**What to DO**
 
Reply within 24 hours
 
Thank them genuinely for their time and consideration.
Keep it short and polite.
